About

Set in the heart of Tel Aviv, OFFSET is a celebration of music, energy, and escape. Bringing people together through disco culture, the festival creates a space where everyday pressure disappears and the night becomes all about movement, freedom, and shared experience.

Community

More than a festival, OFFSET is a gathering of people who live for movement, sound, and shared experiences. A community built around self-expression, connection, and the idea that sometimes all we need is one night to step away from routine and feel completely present.
